Our story

From first job to 6 reviews

The first bathroom Brett tiled was in 2003. Twenty-three years later, BMH Tiling & Bathrooms still operates the same way: one job at a time, done properly. Kath Hawkes had to book Brett months in advance because that is how his schedule works. When the day came, "he turned up on time and everything went like clockwork."

The work covers residential bathrooms, ensuites, laundries, kitchens, and feature walls. But Brett also handles commercial jobs. Stewart Trading brought him in for a warehouse and office complex, and described the tiling as "exceptional" and "outstanding."

Every wet area gets a waterproofing membrane with certificate. Every job is fully licensed and insured. Julie Jones got a custom dog wash tiled into her laundry and said even the other tradesmen on site commented on the finish. That is the standard.
