Our story

From first job to 3 reviews

It started with one van, one trowel, and a bathroom renovation that needed to be right. Billy built Billie J's Tiling by doing the kind of work people talk about. Leslie Mas called his attention to detail "second to none." Nelly Jalz called him "hands down the best."

Today the business runs two branded vans across Ballarat and western Melbourne. The team handles everything from large format floor tiling and designer bathroom fit-outs to full pool tiling and natural stone cladding. They work alongside builders like Hadlex Built on high-end residential projects where zero lippage and continuous grout lines are the standard, not the exception.

Every wet area gets a waterproofing membrane with certificate. Every job gets cleaned up before the team leaves. That is how three out of three customers gave five stars.
