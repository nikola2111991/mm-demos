Our story

From first job to 11 reviews

The first job was a small bathroom in Diggers Rest. Measure twice, cut once, leave the site cleaner than you found it. That was the rule from day one.

Six years and 11 five star reviews later, the rule has not changed. Nicholas Bero hired Sukhdev for a full house retile and said he made his home beautiful. Stuart McLeod booked him for a bathroom renovation, then came back for the ensuite, kitchen, and laundry.

Every wet area gets an AS1547 compliant waterproofing certificate. Every quote is fixed price, given on site after measuring up. No surprises, no hidden costs.
