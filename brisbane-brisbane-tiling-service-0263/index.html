Common questions

Floor tiling runs $50 to $90 AUD per square metre installed. Wall tiling is $55 to $100 per square metre. A full small bathroom retile (about 4m2) costs $2,500 to $5,000 including waterproofing. Larger bathrooms (8m2+) range from $5,000 to $12,000. Bobby quotes on site after measuring up.
7-year waterproofing warranty on all wet areas. Every shower, bathroom, and laundry gets a waterproofing membrane applied to AS1547 standards. You receive a waterproofing certificate on completion.
Most bathroom retiles take 3 to 5 days. Waterproofing needs 24 hours to cure before tiling starts. After grouting, another 24 to 48 hours before you can use the shower. Bobby gives you a timeline before the job starts.
Yes. Every wet area gets an AS1547-compliant waterproofing certificate. This is required by Queensland building standards and protects you if you sell the property or make an insurance claim.
Talk to Bobby about payment options. Typically a deposit before materials are ordered and the balance on completion. For larger jobs, progress payments can be arranged.
Yes, every day. Multiple customers mention it in reviews. Sally Kehoe wrote: they cleaned up each day, which was appreciated. Your home stays liveable while the tiling is being done.
If a tile cracks due to workmanship within the warranty period, Bobby replaces it at no cost. Cracking from structural movement or impact is assessed case by case. Most cracking is preventable with proper substrate preparation and the right adhesive.
