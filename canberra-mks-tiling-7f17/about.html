Our story

From first job to 4 reviews

A bathroom with water damage behind the tiles. The homeowner had no idea until the floor started lifting. Mark stripped it back, waterproofed the substrate, and retiled the entire room in four days. That was 2012.

Fourteen years later, the approach has not changed. Mark still measures twice, still insists on AS1547 compliant waterproofing membrane on every wet area, still hands over the certificate when the job is done. Emily Monteith hired him for a full bathroom renovation plus kitchen and living area floors. She wrote that his meticulous attention to detail meant the renovation greatly exceeded her expectations, finishing on schedule with the property left in pristine condition.

Sed Crest put it simply: Mark is a perfectionist with precise tolerances who communicates clearly and honestly. That is not a marketing line. It is what customers write when they are not being asked to.
