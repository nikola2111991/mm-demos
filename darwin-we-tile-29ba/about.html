A bathroom renovation that looks perfect on day one is easy. One that still looks perfect after five years of daily use, that takes proper technique.

From a waterfront restaurant fit-out in Darwin City to home bathroom renovations across the Top End, the standard stays the same. Tegan Blockey put it simply: the tiling is immaculate and the care shows in every detail.
