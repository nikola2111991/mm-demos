Floor tiling runs $50 to $90 AUD per square metre installed. Wall tiling is $55 to $100 per square metre. A full small bathroom retile (about 4m2) costs $2,500 to $5,000 including waterproofing. Larger bathrooms (8m2+) range from $5,000 to $12,000. John quotes on site after measuring up.
7-year waterproofing warranty on all wet areas. Every shower, bathroom, and laundry gets a waterproofing membrane applied to AS1547 standards. You receive a waterproofing certificate on completion.
Most bathroom retiles take 3 to 5 days. Tarjei Kirkesaether's ensuite took 3 weeks for a full renovation with design changes. After grouting, 24 to 48 hours before you can use the shower. John gives you a timeline before starting.
Yes. Every wet area gets an AS1547-compliant waterproofing certificate. Required by Victorian building standards and protects you if you sell the property or make an insurance claim.
