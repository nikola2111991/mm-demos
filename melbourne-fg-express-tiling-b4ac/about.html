Our story

From first job to 17 reviews

It started with a bathroom in Deer Park. Farhad and Mario spent three days getting every tile line dead straight before grouting. The client told their neighbour, the neighbour called, and that became the next job.

Seven years later, FG Express Tiling has grown from bathrooms to 120m² whole-home retiles, pool surrounds across Melbourne's west, and waterproofing jobs that come with an AS1547 certificate and 7-year warranty. Mario still measures every cut twice. Farhad still answers the phone himself.

Seventeen customers have left five-star reviews. Five of them used the word "recommend." Three mentioned the pricing. Nobody has complained.

"Mario did 120m² of 600x600 tiling throughout our home. Straight lines, perfectly laid out. Very competitive pricing. We almost feel like we ripped him off.". Derek & Stacey Nelson
