Common questions

Wall tiling runs $55 to $100 per square metre installed, floor tiling $50 to $90 per square metre. A small 4m2 bathroom retile starts at $2,500, larger 8m2+ bathrooms $5,000 to $12,000. Ravi provides a fixed-price quote after measuring up, so you know the full cost before work starts.
Every wet area gets a 7-year waterproofing warranty. Ravi applies AS1547 compliant waterproofing membrane and provides a waterproofing certificate on completion. This is not optional, it is included in every bathroom job.
A full bathroom retile takes 3 to 7 days depending on size. After tiling, grout needs 24 hours to cure and waterproofing needs 48 hours before the shower is used. Ravi gives you a timeline before starting so you can plan around it.
Yes. A waterproofing certificate is issued on every job where membrane is applied. This is required by Australian Standards (AS1547) and protects you if you sell the property or make an insurance claim.

Tile cracking after installation usually means a substrate or adhesive issue. Ravi uses modified thinset adhesive and proper levelling to prevent this. If a tile does crack due to workmanship, it is replaced at no charge.
