Our story

From first job to 50 reviews

Kevin needed his family bathroom renovated on a tight deadline. He found Ash and Matt, walked into the Booragoon showroom, picked his tiles, and got a quote the same week. The job finished early.

Direct Tile & Bath started in 2015 as a factory-direct tile importer with a showroom in Booragoon. Then customers started asking: can you install them too? Ash and Matt built a renovation team: tilers, plumbers, electrician, all under one roof. Guy had both bathrooms, laundry, and all flooring done in one job. Kate had two bathrooms renovated, one converted from a laundry.

Fifty customers have left reviews. J H said his major renovation far exceeded expectations. Elly was absolutely thrilled. The showroom is still there: walk in, pick your tiles and tapware, meet the team, and leave with a fixed price quote.

Ash and Matt were a pleasure to deal with from quoting stage to completion. Good, honest helpful advice. We had a narrow timeline and the works were completed earlier than needed. All trades on time, clean and tidy, no issues at all.
