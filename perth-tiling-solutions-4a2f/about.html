Our story

From first job to 50 reviews

Jerry got a call from a homeowner in Noranda whose tiles were lifting off the bathroom floor. The previous tiler had skipped the levelling compound, laid tiles straight onto uneven substrate. Two hours into the strip and retile, Jerry found the waterproofing membrane had never been applied. That job took three days longer than quoted. Jerry charged the original price.

Since then, Perth Tiling Solutions has completed hundreds of bathrooms, kitchens, and renovation projects across Perth. The team now includes James and Wong, each bringing precision with large format tiles, natural stone, and wet area waterproofing.

Sue Rizzi put it simply: "His boys did an amazing job at a very fair price. The tiling was perfect." That is the standard. Every substrate levelled. Every membrane certified. Every grout line aligned.

"The guys were definitely some of the best trades I've used. The work was very good quality, they worked hard and were a pleasure to have in our house.". Ben Rayner, bathroom and powder room renovation
