Our story

From first job to 17 reviews

The first project was a small bathroom in Guildford. Strip the old tiles, waterproof the substrate, lay new porcelain, grout, seal. Sayed measured twice, cut once, and the client wrote a review the same week.

That was 2020. Since then, the jobs got bigger. Full house renovations in North Sydney. Five bedroom weatherboard repaints with bathroom retiling in Dover Heights. Kitchen splashbacks in Parramatta. Each one finished on time, within budget, with the same attention to detail that Ian Hayles described: "every detail was executed with care and precision."

Today, 17 customers have left reviews. Every single one gave 5 stars. Not because anyone asked, but because the grout lines were straight, the waterproofing certificate was in their inbox before the final coat dried, and the site was cleaner when the team left than when they arrived.
